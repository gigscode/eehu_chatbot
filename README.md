# I made eehu! to help businesses keep track of their leads

When a customer asks for a price or a quote, it is easy for that chat to get buried in WhatsApp. If nobody follows up, a real business opportunity can be lost.

eehu! gives service businesses a simple way to capture enquiries from their website, keep the details together, and remember who needs a reply.

## How it works

1. A visitor starts a conversation through the chat on your website.
2. When they want to speak with your team or continue on WhatsApp, eehu! asks for their name, contact details, and what they need.
3. eehu! saves those details with the conversation, so your team can find the enquiry in the dashboard.
4. Your team can update the lead's status, add a next step, set a follow-up date, and record the deal value when it is won.

The form the visitor fills in is part of the chat on your website. It is not a backend screen. The form sends the details to eehu!'s backend, which validates and saves them to the conversation record.

## What your team can keep track of

- Who made an enquiry and how to contact them
- What they asked about
- The conversation with the visitor
- Whether the lead is new, contacted, quoted, won, or lost
- What to do next and when to follow up
- The value of deals you win

eehu! is for businesses where customers ask questions, request quotes, or book a consultation before deciding. That includes professional services, contractors, clinics, agencies, and event businesses.

## Getting started

We set up eehu! for your business and add it to your existing website. You do not need to rebuild your site. It works with WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Squarespace, Wix, and custom websites.
