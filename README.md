# eehu!

**The website enquiry-to-customer system for businesses that sell through consultations, quotations, or WhatsApp.**

---

## The costly leak on your website

Every day, potential clients visit your website. They look at your work, read through your services, and hesitate right before reaching out.

Maybe they want to know if you can take on their project this month. Maybe they need a quick price estimate before committing. Or maybe they simply want to confirm that you work in their specific location.

When high-value prospective clients cannot get a direct answer within moments, they leave. They click the back button and contact the next firm on Google.

For businesses that sell high-ticket services (such as interior designers, clinics, solar installers, agencies, consultants, and contractors), losing just two or three of these enquiries every month can mean losing thousands in revenue.

**eehu! solves this problem.**

---

## Not just a chat widget. An enquiry pipeline.

Most tools either offer simple live chat that requires someone glued to a desk all day, or robotic bots that frustrate visitors with useless loops.

eehu! is built around one clear goal: **turn website visitors into qualified leads, keep track of who follows up, and record which conversations turn into paying customers.**

### 1. Catch hesitant buyers before they leave
When a visitor is lingering on your service or pricing page, eehu! invites them into a quick conversation. It answers baseline questions about how you work, turnaround times, and general requirements so visitors feel confident.

### 2. Qualify the enquiry before handoff
When someone is ready to talk business or request a quote, eehu! collects their essentials first:
- Their full name.
- Their WhatsApp number or email address.
- Exactly what service or quotation they need.

Even if they step away or their phone battery dies, your business keeps the lead details safe in your database.

### 3. Route hot leads directly to WhatsApp
Once qualified, the visitor is handed off to your team on WhatsApp with their details already pre-filled. No awkward introductions. Your sales team knows their name and exactly what they need before saying a word.

### 4. Track every lead to cash in your dashboard
Once a conversation moves to WhatsApp, WhatsApp itself is a private, encrypted app. No website can automatically spy on what is said inside WhatsApp or automatically record when someone pays through a bank transfer.

eehu! solves this honestly and simply with an enquiry pipeline inside your private admin dashboard:
- **Status tracking:** Update each enquiry as `New`, `Contacted`, `Quote sent`, `Won`, or `Lost`.
- **Follow-up accountability:** Immediately see which enquiries have been sitting for more than two hours without a follow-up from your team.
- **Revenue logging:** When a deal closes, record the deal value so you can see exactly how much revenue originated from website conversations.
- **Audience signals:** Read past chat transcripts to discover the recurring questions your website fails to explain clearly.
