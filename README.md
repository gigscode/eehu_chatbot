## The five things that make eehu! work

### 1. A proper lead record
When someone wants a consultation or a quote, eehu! collects the important details before opening WhatsApp:

- Full name
- WhatsApp number or email address
- What they need or the quote they want
- Full conversation history

Even if the visitor closes the browser or forgets to send the message on WhatsApp, your business still has the lead record in your database.

### 2. A follow-up queue that drives action
Inside your admin dashboard, you can filter enquiries by what matters today:

- **Needs Action Today:** New enquiries that still need a reply, plus overdue follow-ups and quotes waiting for a decision.
- **Active Pipeline:** Conversations currently in progress such as `Contacted` or `Quote sent`.
- **Won Deals:** Closed clients and total revenue generated.

### 3. Clear next steps and due dates
You do not need to rely on memory. For every enquiry, your team can:

- Write the exact next action, such as *"Send revised proposal"* or *"Call about site inspection"*.
- Choose a follow-up date with one click (`Today`, `Tomorrow`, or a specific date).
- See alerts when an enquiry is due or overdue.

### 4. A clean sales pipeline
Track every conversation across five stages:
`New` &rarr; `Contacted` &rarr; `Quote sent` &rarr; `Won` &rarr; `Lost`

When a deal is won, enter the amount in your local currency. eehu! then calculates the total revenue your website conversations have brought in.

### 5. Useful audience insight
Read real conversations from visitors who hesitated. Learn the exact questions, concerns, and objections your website is not answering well, so you can improve your message and close more deals.
