# Project instructions

- Do not use em dash characters in project copy, UI text, metadata, comments, or documentation. Use a period, comma, colon, or parentheses instead.
- Write for real human needs and clear business pain points. Prefer specific, plain language over generic AI marketing language.
- Avoid filler claims such as "revolutionize", "seamless", "next-generation", or "powered by AI" unless the product behavior makes the claim concrete.
